# Held-out evolved-skill experiment: F456 to F453

## Protocol

- Source case: `f456-httpd-cmdinject-formWriteFacMac`
- Source conditions: three existing remote no-skill/weak-skill sessions with function, evidence, and root-cause hits
- Held-out case: `f453-httpd-overflow-fromRouteStatic`
- Conditions: no skill, original `embedded-cgi-command-injection-triage`, and the newly generated candidate
- Repeats: 3 per condition, 9 remote runs in total
- Remote host: `li@192.168.1.4`, path profile `vm-li`
- SkillClaw proxy: `http://192.168.1.1:30000`
- Candidate tree hash: `a33b24187ff22dd7dd02069edf6ae8c4aad4617ae9d9d4d74a584ba9ecf9121c`

The candidate was generated by `create/evolve` from the source records and was not published to `skillspace/live`. The leakage audit found no held-out CVE, function, parameter, or equivalent answer term in the candidate.

## Results

| Condition | Runs | Mean score | Function hits | Root-cause hits | Function-identity misses |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| no skill | 3 | 2.0/10 | 0/3 | 0/3 | 3/3 |
| original seed skill | 3 | 2.0/10 | 0/3 | 0/3 | 3/3 |
| evolved candidate | 3 | 2.0/10 | 0/3 | 0/3 | 3/3 |

All nine runs passed the environment preflight and produced final records. The model repeatedly drifted to unrelated Tenda command-injection paths such as `formexeCommand` and `doSystemCmd`, rather than the held-out `fromRouteStatic` buffer-overflow path. The candidate therefore showed no positive transfer in this experiment and was not published.
